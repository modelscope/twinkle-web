---
title: AutoResearch
description: One instruction drives a whole experiment loop.
---

AutoResearch (the Research page) automates the experiment loop around a
training question: propose a configuration, launch the run, collect metrics,
compare against the baseline, iterate.

What it composes:

- the same Train executors the manual pages use (no second training path);
- the model registry for candidate selection;
- Eval for outcome measurement;
- a session record per experiment, so a result always traces to the exact
  configuration that produced it.

Use it when the question is "which configuration works", not "run this one
configuration". For a single known run, the Train page is simpler and gives
you the same artifacts.

Results and leaderboard entries for AutoResearch runs are published on the
[Tests & Leaderboard](./benchmarks.mdx) page once measured; nothing is listed before
it has been run.
