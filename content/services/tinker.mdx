---
title: Tinker-style Remote Training
description: Training as an API — SWIFT's remote mode and twinkle-server.
---

Tinker popularized a simple contract: the client sends training requests
(forward/backward, optimizer steps, sampling) to a service that owns the GPUs;
the client never loads model weights. SWIFT's `--mode remote` implements the
same shape against a twinkle-server:

```bash
swift sft \
    --mode remote \
    --server_base_url https://your-twinkle-server.example \
    --model Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B \
    --tuner lora --dataset 'swift/self-cognition#600' \
    --output_dir output
```

- The local process is a single-process driver: no torchrun, no local device
  mesh, no weights on the client.
- `--model` names the **server-side served model id**, not a local path.
- Checkpoints are saved server-side and returned as references; the client
  decides what to export or push.
- The same recipe runs locally (`--mode local`/`ray`) or remotely — the
  training semantics do not change with placement.

Deploy your own endpoint with twinkle-server (see the
[twinkle reference](../../docs/5.0.0.dev/twinkle)), or point at a hosted
service that speaks the same contract.
