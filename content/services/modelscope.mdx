title: ModelScope
description: The model hub and compute platform the stack integrates with.
---

[ModelScope](https://modelscope.cn) is the default hub for SWIFT: `--model`
and `--dataset` ids resolve there unless `--use_hf true` switches to Hugging
Face. Weights cache under `MODELSCOPE_CACHE`.

Platform services relevant to training:

- **Model hub** — the registered model families ship with ModelScope ids;
  day-0 support means new models are trainable as soon as they land.
- **Notebook / compute** — hosted GPU environments for running the examples
  without local hardware (see the community resources in the ms-swift README).
- **Studio deployment** — serving deployed models; account and billing
  operations live on the platform, not in this documentation.
